Stores product information.

| Column | Data Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ProdID | INT | Unique product ID |

| OrderDate | DATE | Order date |

# 6. Part A – Whole-Table Aggregates

Part A contains **10 tasks (A1–A10)**.

### A1 – Total Records

Counts total customers, products, and orders using `COUNT()` and subqueries.

### A2 – Minimum and Maximum Price

Finds the lowest and highest product prices using:

```sql
MIN()
MAX()
```

### A3 – Average Product Price

Calculates the average product price using:

```sql
AVG()
ROUND()
```

### A4 – Total Stock

Calculates the total available product stock using:

```sql
SUM()
```

### A5 – Distinct Cities

Counts different customer cities while ignoring duplicate values and NULL values.

Uses:

```sql
COUNT(DISTINCT City)
```

### A6 – Distinct Categories

Counts the number of unique product categories.

### A7 – City Data Availability

Counts customers with a city and separately counts customers whose city is NULL.

### A8 – Order Date Range

Finds the earliest and latest order dates using:

```sql
MIN()
MAX()
```

### A9 – Total Revenue

Calculates total revenue from:

```text
Quantity × Price
```

It uses an `INNER JOIN` between `OrderItem` and `Product`.

### A10 – Average Order Quantity

Calculates the average quantity ordered per order.

# 7. Part B – GROUP BY, HAVING & Aggregates with Joins

Part B contains **17 tasks (B1–B17)**.

### B1 – Customers by City

Groups customers according to their city and counts the customers in each city.

### B2 – Products by Category

Counts products in each category.

### B3 – Category Price Analysis

Calculates average, minimum, and maximum prices for each category.

### B4 – Stock by Category

Calculates total stock available in each product category.

### B5 – Orders by Year

Groups orders by year and counts the number of orders per year.

### B6 – Orders by Month in 2024

Groups 2024 orders by month.

### B7 – High-Value Categories

Uses `HAVING` to find categories whose average price is greater than 5000.

### B8 – Cities with Multiple Customers

Finds cities having more than one customer.

### B9 – Customer Order Frequency

Uses a `LEFT JOIN` to count orders for every customer, including customers with no orders.

### B10 – Product Sales Quantity

Calculates total quantity sold for each product and keeps products with no sales using `LEFT JOIN` and `COALESCE()`.

### B11 – Revenue by Category

Calculates total revenue generated by each product category.

### B12 – Customer Lifetime Spending

Calculates total spending for each customer, including customers with no activity.

### B13 – High-Spending Customers

Uses `HAVING` to find customers whose total spending is greater than 50,000.

### B14 – City Revenue Analysis

Calculates the number of customers and total revenue for cities having more than one customer.

### B15 – Top-Selling Products

Finds the top three products according to total quantity sold.

Uses:

```sql
ORDER BY
LIMIT 3
```

### B16 – Revenue by Year

Calculates yearly revenue from order data.

### B17 – Average Order Value

Uses a subquery to calculate each order's total value and then finds the average order value.

- Calculate revenue from multiple tables.
- Handle missing values with `COALESCE()`.
- Use subqueries for aggregate calculations.
- Group date information by year and month.
